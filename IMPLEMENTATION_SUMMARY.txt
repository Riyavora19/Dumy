🚀 HOW TO USE:

1. ADMIN PANEL → Budget Plans → "Create Budget Plan"

2. STEP 1: Enter customer details and project info
   - Search existing customer OR enter new customer
   - Auto-fills all details if customer exists
   - Enter project/room name
   - Set estimated budget
   - Add notes/requirements

3. STEP 2: Add products
   - Use search bar to find products
   - Filter by category (Tiles, CP, Bathtub, etc.)
   - Click products to add to cart
   - Adjust quantity and discount for each item
   - Watch budget tracker in real-time
   - Choose action:
     * "Save Budget Plan" → Saves as draft for later
     * "Convert to Order" → Creates order immediately

4. ORDERS → View order → "Generate Quotation PDF"
   - Professional PDF downloads automatically
   - Ready to print or email to customer
